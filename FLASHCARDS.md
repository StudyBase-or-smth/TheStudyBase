# Flashcards

Topic and class editors share the Flashcard Questions section for every layout. Authors can turn off the automatic notes card, include/exclude individual question-and-answer pairs, edit accepted answer variants (separated by `|`), preview both sides, and delete pairs with Undo. Incomplete and duplicate pairs are omitted from study; warnings also suggest splitting long cards. Existing pairs receive stable IDs when normalized and keep them when saved.

Study defaults to all cards with no session limit. Due/new and missed-card filters, unit filtering, optional 5/10/20-card limits, shuffling, keyboard controls, and a completion summary remain available. Goals, hidden cards, and quality warnings sit in the compact More menu. Show the answer, then Next card; Next records the review and schedules it initially in one day and then doubling. Manual Again / Got it / Easy buttons and their number-key shortcuts have been removed. Typed answers compare with the reference or authored variants after whitespace/case/punctuation normalization; learners can self-assess their answer. No generated answers or fuzzy grading are used.

Edit this card opens its source topic when the account has editing access, with a return link to resume the session after saving. Hide for me changes only personal study state; Hidden cards restores cards without editing shared content. Progress, hidden cards, goals, and session positions are cached per account and synchronized to the private `_flashcards_UID` key. Guests keep local progress only. Removing a source card removes it from resumed sessions; new cards appear in the next session. Reviews of the same card count once toward the daily target.

Goals & reminders sets a daily target, timezone, and optional reminder time. Deploy both repositories for reminders. The running backend checks synchronized goals about once a minute and creates standard StudyBase notifications addressed to the owner, including a Study now link. It sends at most one reminder and one completion notification per goal per local day. Reminders are suppressed once the goal is reached. These are in-app notifications, not operating-system push; the backend can schedule them while the browser is closed, and they appear when StudyBase is opened. Unsynced offline progress can delay or make a reminder outdated until the next sync.

Regression checks: `node --test tests` here and in Backend-server-program. Browser interactions are covered with jsdom; live account sync and visual browser validation need a running deployment.
